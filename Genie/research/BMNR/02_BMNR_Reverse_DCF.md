# Reverse DCF Analysis: BitMine Immersion Technologies, Inc. (BMNR)

## Mandatory Assumption Disclosure
This analysis uses:
• Single-stage Gordon Growth Model
• Steady-state economics
• Long-term margin assumptions
• Current capital structure
• No speculative optionality valuation unless disclosed

**Business Profile:** Infrastructure / Semiconductor (Crypto Mining & Staking)
BitMine Immersion Technologies (BMNR) operates in the digital asset infrastructure space, focusing on bitcoin mining, Ethereum staking, and treasury management of digital assets.

---

## Section 1 — Reverse DCF Summary
**What must this company become over the next 5–10 years?**
• **Revenue scale:** Must scale from its current $287M TTM revenue to roughly $4.67B in steady-state annual revenue under base-case assumptions.
• **Market position:** Must become one of the top 3 global infrastructure providers for digital asset computing and staking.
• **Geographic scale:** Requires massive, multi-national data center footprints with access to gigawatts of cheap, renewable power.
• **Margin profile:** Must achieve and sustain long-term Free Cash Flow margins of 20%, an incredibly difficult feat for a capital-intensive hardware business.
• **Competitive position:** Needs to establish a cost-of-capital and power-cost advantage that completely boxes out new entrants and existing giants.

Implied Revenue = EV × (CoE − g) ÷ FCF Margin

## Section 3 — Risk Adjusted Revenue
Risk Adjusted Revenue = Implied Revenue ÷ Probability

| Scenario | Implied Revenue | Probability | Risk Adjusted Revenue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Conservative | $7.366B | 50% | $14.73B |
| Base | $4.675B | 70% | $6.678B |
| Aggressive | $3.060B | 100% | $3.060B |

**Explanation:** Execution risk is extreme. Factoring in a 70% probability of success for the Base scenario, the market is effectively pricing in a risk-adjusted steady-state revenue requirement of over $6.67B.
