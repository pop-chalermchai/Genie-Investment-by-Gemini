### 🎯 3. Business Model & Economic Moat

* **Revenue Architecture (How it makes money):**
    * SK Hynix primarily generates revenue through the design and manufacturing of memory semiconductor products. The high-margin High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) and enterprise SSDs (eSSDs) segments currently act as the tip of the spear, anchoring profitability. Legacy DRAM and NAND form the broader volume base.
* **Competitive Advantage (Economic Moat Analysis):**
    * **Wide Moat (Proprietary Technology & Scale):** Advanced packaging technologies like MR-MUF give SK Hynix a structural yield and thermal performance advantage in HBM. High capital intensity and steep learning curves prevent new entrants from challenging the top three players (SK Hynix, Samsung, Micron).
* **The Consensus Mispricing (The Core Thesis):**
    * The market still views SK Hynix through the lens of cyclical commodity memory. However, HBM is increasingly acting as a customized, logic-like business with long-term locked contracts, fundamentally altering the terminal margin profile and cycle volatility.
