### 🎯 2. Investment Thesis
*At a current stock price of ~$227.81 ($57.84 Billion market capitalization), Nebius Group N.V. represents a high-conviction AI play, but the entry valuation has no margin of safety. We recommend an **AVOID / WAIT & WATCH** rating due to structural dilution risks and capital intensity.*

* **The Capital Dilution Trap:** Building large-scale AI factories requires massive capital expenditure. With CapEx projected at $20–$25 Billion in 2026, Nebius is burning cash rapidly. Even with $9.3 Billion cash on hand, they will need to issue more shares, directly diluting existing shareholders and lowering per-share returns.
* **GPU Supply Moat & Nvidia Backing:** Nebius holds a unique position as one of the few providers with direct, large-scale allocations of Nvidia's H100, H200, and Blackwell GPU architectures. This supply relationship acts as a strong competitive moat against smaller tier-2 cloud service providers.
* **Hyperscaler Competition:** While Nebius is securing massive deals (including a potential Meta contract), they are competing directly with Microsoft Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, and Oracle. Hyperscalers possess massive cash flow engines that Nebius lacks, enabling them to easily match or undercut Nebius's pricing over a long-term cycle.
