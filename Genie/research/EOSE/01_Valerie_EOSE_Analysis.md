### 🎯 3. Business Model & Economic Moat

* **Revenue Architecture (How it makes money):**
    * *85% of revenue is derived from utility-scale energy storage systems (Znyth™ zinc-based battery systems), and 15% from engineering, services, and software (DawnOS™ energy management platform).*
* **Competitive Advantage (Economic Moat Analysis):**
    * *Narrow moat based on proprietary Znyth™ aqueous zinc-hybrid chemistry. The water-based electrolyte is inherently non-flammable, eliminating thermal runaway risk and the need for expensive HVAC/cooling or fire-suppression systems. It utilizes earth-abundant, non-toxic, recyclable materials (free of lithium, cobalt, or nickel), providing a highly secure domestic supply chain and structural cost advantages.*
* **The Consensus Mispricing (The Core Thesis):**
    * *The broader market treats EOSE as a highly speculative pre-revenue developer. This overlooks the commercial ramp of Thorn Hill Line 2 and the 2 GWh capacity reservation under the Frontier Power JV with Cerberus Capital. As manufacturing scales and automation drives unit costs down, gross margins will flip positive, prompting a major market re-rating.*
