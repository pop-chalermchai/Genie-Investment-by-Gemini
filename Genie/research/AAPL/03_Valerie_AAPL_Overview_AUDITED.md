### 🎯 3. Business Model & Economic Moat

* **Revenue Architecture (How it makes money):**
    * Core revenue comes from premium hardware (iPhone, Mac, iPad, Wearables), heavily supported by a high-margin, recurring Services segment (App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay) which now accounts for a significant portion of gross profit.
* **Competitive Advantage (Economic Moat Analysis):**
    * **Wide Moat:** Extremely high switching costs due to the iOS ecosystem lock-in. Unmatched brand equity and massive network effects via the App Store. Competitors struggle to replicate the seamless hardware-software integration.
* **The Consensus Mispricing (The Core Thesis):**
    * The market may be slightly overestimating near-term hardware upgrade cycles while underestimating the long-term margin expansion potential as Services outpace hardware sales.
