# Apple (AAPL) Q2 2026 Earnings Summary

## Executive Summary
Apple reported its fiscal 2026 second-quarter results on April 30, 2026. The results significantly surpassed Wall Street expectations, showcasing strong growth across multiple segments.

## Key Financial Metrics
- **Total Revenue:** $111.2 billion (up 16.6%–17% year-over-year)
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** $2.01 (beating consensus estimates of $1.94, a 22% increase YoY)
- **Services Revenue:** A record-breaking $30.98 billion.
- **Greater China Performance:** Strong rebound with revenue jumping 28% in the region.

## Capital Returns Program
- **Share Repurchases:** The board authorized an additional **$100 billion** for share buybacks.
- **Dividends:** Quarterly dividend increased by 4% to **$0.27 per share**, paid out on May 14, 2026.

## Product Highlights
The quarter's performance was largely driven by robust demand for the iPhone 17 lineup and continued expansion of the Services ecosystem (including Apple Music, iCloud, Apple TV+, and App Store revenues).
