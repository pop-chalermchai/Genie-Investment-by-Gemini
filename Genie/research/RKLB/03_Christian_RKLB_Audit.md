### B3 — Section 2: Implied Revenue Mathematics

**Verdict: PASS**

The Gordon Growth Model formula applied:

**Implied Revenue = EV × (CoE – g) / FCF Margin**

All three scenarios independently recalculated:

| Scenario | CoE | g | FCF Margin | Recalculated Implied Revenue | Valerie's Figure | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Conservative | 16% | 3% | 15% | $60.74B × 0.13 / 0.15 = **$52.64B** | $52.6B | ✓ PASS |
| Base | 14% | 3% | 20% | $60.74B × 0.11 / 0.20 = **$33.41B** | $33.4B | ✓ PASS |
| Aggressive | 12% | 3% | 25% | $60.74B × 0.09 / 0.25 = **$21.87B** | $21.9B | ✓ PASS |

Mathematics are internally consistent and correctly applied.

**WACC / CoE Assumption Assessment:**

The report uses Cost of Equity (CoE) rather than WACC. Given RKLB's negligible financial leverage (debt/equity = 0.06), CoE ≈ WACC in this context — a reasonable simplification, correctly disclosed.

CoE range of 12%–16% is defensible:
- Risk-free rate (10Y UST): ~4.4–4.7% (June 2026)
- Equity risk premium: ~5.5%
- Beta: 2.50 (confirmed on StockAnalysis)
- CAPM implied CoE: 4.5% + 2.50 × 5.5% = **18.25%**

**Observation:** The report's CoE range of 12–16% is **below the CAPM-implied rate of ~18.25%** using RKLB's confirmed beta of 2.50. Using the CAPM-implied rate would produce materially higher implied revenue requirements than stated:

| Scenario | CAPM CoE (~18.25%) | g | FCF Margin | CAPM-Implied Revenue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Conservative | 18.25% | 3% | 15% | $60.74B × 0.1525 / 0.15 = **$61.75B** |
| Base | 18.25% | 3% | 20% | $60.74B × 0.1525 / 0.20 = **$46.31B** |
| Aggressive | 18.25% | 3% | 25% | $60.74B × 0.1525 / 0.25 = **$37.05B** |

**Conclusion on WACC:** Valerie's CoE assumptions (12–16%) appear to reflect investor-applied discount rates rather than strict CAPM outputs. This is a recognized analytical judgment call — practitioners frequently apply lower rates for high-growth firms to reflect the market's actual required return rather than mechanical CAPM. However, the report should explicitly acknowledge that the CAPM-implied rate (~18%) would produce even more extreme implied revenue targets than those stated. As currently written, the conservative-to-aggressive CoE range may inadvertently make the math appear more generous than a fully rigorous CAPM application.

**Assessment: Not a mathematical error. A disclosure gap requiring clarification.**
