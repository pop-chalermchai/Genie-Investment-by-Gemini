### 🎯 3. Business Model & Economic Moat

* **Revenue Architecture (How it makes money):**
    * *Amkor generates revenue primarily as an Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test (OSAT) provider. It is seeing significant growth from advanced packaging (2.5D, high-density fan-out, SiP) catering to AI, HPC, and premium smartphones.*
* **Competitive Advantage (Economic Moat Analysis):**
    * *Narrow Moat. The moat stems from high switching costs, deep integration with tier-1 foundries (like TSMC), and significant capital barriers to entry. The 10-year strategic partnership with TSMC in Arizona strengthens its ecosystem lock-in.*
* **The Consensus Mispricing (The Core Thesis):**
    * *The market may be overestimating the near-term margin expansion, ignoring the drag from heavy CapEx and depreciation as new facilities (like Arizona) ramp up. The revenue growth narrative is correct, but execution on FCF generation remains a show-me story.*
