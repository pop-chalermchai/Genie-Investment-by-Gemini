### 🎯 3. Business Model & Economic Moat

* **Revenue Architecture (How it makes money):**
    * Core revenue is derived from the design and manufacture of high-performance optical and photonic components (EMLs, Optical Circuit Switches, transceivers) essential for hyperscale AI data centers.
* **Competitive Advantage (Economic Moat Analysis):**
    * *Narrow to Moderate Moat.* Built on deep expertise in indium phosphide (InP) photonic integration and proprietary manufacturing at scale. Its strategic partnership and multi-billion-dollar supply agreements with NVIDIA create high switching costs and formidable barriers to entry.
* **The Consensus Mispricing (The Core Thesis):**
    * The market believes Lumentum is the undisputable optical winner in the AI race. However, the current valuation prices in near-monopoly margins and market share, ignoring the rising threat of silicon photonics competitors and the cyclical nature of hardware deployments.
