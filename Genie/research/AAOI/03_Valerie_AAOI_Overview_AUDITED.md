### 🎯 3. Business Model & Economic Moat

* **Revenue Architecture (How it makes money):**
    * *The company designs and manufactures optical communication products, specifically high-speed 400G, 800G, and upcoming 1.6T optical transceivers. These components are essential for interconnectivity in massive AI GPU clusters.*
* **Competitive Advantage (Economic Moat Analysis):**
    * *Narrow Moat. AAOI's primary advantage is its vertical integration, controlling the production cycle down to its own laser chips. This provides supply chain reliability for hyperscale cloud operators (Amazon, Microsoft) seeking to de-risk against reliance on outsourced components.*
* **The Consensus Mispricing (The Core Thesis):**
    * *The market is pricing AAOI as a dominant long-term monopoly in AI transceivers. While the structural demand is undeniable, the valuation implies that AAOI will maintain high margins and capture massive market share without experiencing pricing compression from larger competitors like Coherent or InnoLight.*
