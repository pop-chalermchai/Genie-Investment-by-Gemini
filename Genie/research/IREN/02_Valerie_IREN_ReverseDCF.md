$$\text{Asymmetric Ratio} = \frac{\text{Bull Case Upside}}{\text{Bear Case Downside}} = \frac{\$84.50 - \$54.35}{\$54.35 - \$12.00} = \frac{+\$30.15}{-\$42.35} = 0.71\text{x}$$

**Verdict:** 🔴 **AVOID / HOLD**. The calculated payoff ratio is **0.71x**, which falls critically short of our mandatory **3.0x** threshold. The risk-reward ratio is unfavorable at this valuation.
