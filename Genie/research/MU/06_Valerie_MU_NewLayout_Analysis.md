### 🛑 5. The "Bear Case" Summary (Tail Risks)

*   **Thesis-Breaker Risk:** 
    *   Cyclical Over-CapEx and Competitor Price War. Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron are in a massive CapEx race. If Samsung aggressively floods the HBM and advanced DDR5 markets in late 2026 or 2027 to reclaim market share, average selling prices (ASPs) will plummet. This would compress Micron's peak gross margins of 74.9% down to legacy mid-cycle averages of 35-40%, compressing FCF to a cyclical floor of $3.5 Billion and wiping out the premium secular valuation multiples.
*   **Advanced Packaging and Transition Yield Risks:**
    *   Stacking 12 to 16 DRAM dies for HBM4 utilizing TSMC 3nm/5nm and hybrid bonding is highly complex. A single defective die in a stack renders the entire module useless. Poor yields would result in massive write-downs and lost market share.
*   **Extreme Customer Concentration:**
    *   Micron's sales are heavily concentrated among NVIDIA and a few US hyperscalers (Microsoft, AWS, Meta, Google). Any digestion cycle in GPU orders or slowdown in hyperscaler CapEx would trigger immediate and severe contract cancellations.
